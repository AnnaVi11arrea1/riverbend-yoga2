Pricing Overview

Our class pricing is designed to be flexible and accessible. We offer drop-in rates, class packages, and membership options to suit your needs. Request a spot today on the Events page to get in touch and learn about our offerings.

Membership Options

We offer various membership options for those who wish to attend classes regularly, providing both convenience and savings.

  • Drop-in Rate: Pay per class without any long-term commitment.
  • Monthly Membership: Unlimited classes for a fixed monthly fee.
  • Quarterly Membership: Discounted rate for a three-month commitment.
  • Annual Membership: Best value for dedicated practitioners attending classes throughout the year.

Note: We welcome all prospective members to try our classes for free with a complimentary trial session!
